# Design System: Rich Vagos

## Colors
- **Core Strategy**: Committed (Black/Red/White).
- **Primary**: `oklch(55% 0.25 25)` (Rich Red)
- **Neutral High**: `oklch(98% 0.01 25)` (Off-white)
- **Neutral Low**: `oklch(15% 0.02 25)` (Deep Black/Zinc)
- **Accent**: `oklch(60% 0.15 25)` (Lighter Red for highlights)

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: Bold, italicized, or heavy sans-serif (e.g., Inter Black, Oswald).
- **Body Font**: Clean, readable sans-serif (e.g., Inter Medium).
- **Scale**: Large headings (4xl+), distinct hierarchy.

## Spacing & Rhythm
- **Rhythm**: Varied spacing, larger gaps for breathability.
- **Radius**: Large, distinct corner radii (like `rounded-tr-3xl`) used purposefully.

## Components
- **Cards**: High contrast, bold borders or solid fills, hover states with scaling and color shifts.
- **Sections**: Full-width backgrounds to create visual "beats" on the page.
