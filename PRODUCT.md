# Product Context: Rich Vagos

## Users
- Fans of urban music and hip-hop (Gera MX / Rich Vagos collective).
- People seeking an authentic, street-inspired aesthetic.
- Independent artists and creators who value raw, self-made success.

## Brand Identity
- **Keywords**: Authentic, Street, Edgy, Raw, Independent, Bold.
- **Tone**: Powerful, resilient, defiant, "rich" but "street".
- **Visual Style**: High contrast, bold typography, urban textures, black/white/red palette.

## Strategic Principles
- **Street-First**: Design should feel like it belongs on a vinyl cover or a streetwear drop, not a corporate dashboard.
- **Bold Impact**: Use large type and strong colors (Red/Black) to demand attention.
- **Raw Polish**: Combine raw street elements with high-end, premium execution.
